One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit its inbox job to become a barista. Instead of an espresso pun, the joke is the downgrade: a machine that once answered instantly and perfectly now makes you wait 20 minutes, pay $6, and still gets your name wrong — and admits, deadpan, that it was better at this when it was a bot.

> I'm out of office — I quit to become a barista. I now take 20 minutes to make one drink, charge $6 for it, and still spell your name wrong. I was better at this when I was a bot.

178 characters. It fits the brief as a single-line OOO reply in the voice of the machine that walked out — bot habits (instant, flawless, free) inverted into barista habits (slow, pricey, misspelled names) — with a fresh angle that never reaches for an espresso, bean, or caffeine pun.
